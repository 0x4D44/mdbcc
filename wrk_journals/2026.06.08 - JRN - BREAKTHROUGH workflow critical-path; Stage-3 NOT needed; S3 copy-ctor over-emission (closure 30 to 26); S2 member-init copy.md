## The breakthrough (workflow synthesis verdict)

**Full Stage-3 virtual-base support is NOT required for a linked railc, and is
NOT on the critical path.** Hard evidence the workflow gathered:

- Only **7 of the 30** unresolveds are referenced DIRECTLY by railc's own objects
  (fixed-string scan of railc/*.o): TApplication/TFrameWindow/TGdiBase/TLayout/
  TDialog ctors + `_hInstance` + `_lrand`. The other 23 are OWL/BIDS/RTL-internal
  cross-references.
- **None of the 7 are vbase-blocked.** TApplication is single-inheritance
  (`:public TModule`); TGdiBase has NO bases; both compile to .o today. TXOwl is
  pure single inheritance (`TXOwl:TXBase:xmsg`) — the "data-bearing vbase" label
  the four investigators independently applied was WRONG.
- The mangled ctor symbols decode as ordinary/copy ctors, not vbase
  constructions. The ONLY genuine vbase case (istream/ostream virtually inherit
  ios) gates FILEDOC/DIB — library files railc never directly constructs.

The real blockers are: (a) **copy-ctor OVER-EMISSION**, (b) one genuinely-missing
def (@TLayout from a copy-ctor-synthesis compile failure), and (c) trivial
_lrand/_hInstance glue — plus a per-file tail.
