# GOAL - Compiler Quality and Oracle Expansion

Date: 2026-06-19
Status: Proposed next quality track

## Goal prompt

Move mdbcc from "we have several strong point oracles" to "we have a
systematic, prioritized compiler-quality map that tells us what to fix next,
why it matters, and which independent oracle will prove it."

The aim is not to import every compiler test suite or chase every TODO. The
aim is to use real programs, external references, generated stress cases, and
structural invariants to expose the highest-value gaps in the compiler, then
turn those gaps into a small ranked work queue.

## Why this matters

mdbcc already has unusually good oracle foundations:

- O1 hand-expected executable tests and byte-identity stripes.
- O2/O3 MSVC and Borland differential tests.
- BCC 4.52 compile/link/resource oracles.
- O12 i386 behavioural ratchets.
- O13/O14 object/linker structural oracles.
- O15 real-header preprocess and parse acceptance ratchets.
- BRC/resource byte-differentials.
- RailC and stock OWL product-path smoke tests.
- Build timing and per-phase instrumentation.

The gap is not "we need tests". The gap is that the emitted signal is spread
across many suites, ignored tests, scratchpad notes, journals, and one-off
probe reports. The next quality step is to unify that signal, expand it with a
small number of high-leverage external oracles, and rank the work by unlock
value rather than by whichever failure is currently loudest.

## Stakeholders and quality bars

The people who matter for this goal:

- Arthur, who needs a trustworthy picture of what the compiler can and cannot
  handle.
- Future agents, who need a clear next action without re-discovering the same
  failure landscape.
- Users of the eventual toolchain, who care that real Borland-era C/C++ and
  OWL programs rebuild and behave correctly on modern Windows.
- Maintainers, including us, who need the large parser/codegen surfaces to stop
  being collision magnets.

Quality bars:

- A failure must be reproducible, classified, and tied to an oracle.
- A ranked backlog item must name the real program or corpus it unblocks.
- A green test must not mean "we skipped the meaningful oracle silently".
- Random or external-suite failures must be reduced or otherwise actionable
  before they enter the bug backlog.
- Structural refactors must preserve byte identity or have an explicit
  behaviour-changing reason.

## North-star acceptance

This goal is done when:

1. There is a generated quality map that combines the major oracle outputs into
   one ranked compiler backlog.
2. The stock BC4.52 OWL product sweep covers more than the current BUTTON and
   INSTANCE examples and classifies failures by phase: preprocess, parse,
   codegen, link, launch, window smoke, dialog smoke, and close.
3. At least one external runnable corpus adapter is integrated in a small,
   curated, non-noisy form.
4. At least one generated ABI/calling-convention torture suite exists and has
   found or pinned a real class of historical bugs.
5. At least one fuzzing path is proven end to end: generate, compile/run
   differentially, reduce, and turn the reduced case into a stable regression.
6. Structural invariants cover at least the highest-risk object/runtime shapes:
   relocations, symbol uniqueness, section alignment, vtable/RTTI layout, stack
   alignment, and call-frame bookkeeping.
7. The first ranked quality map has driven at least three fixes or deliberate
   deferrals, with before/after oracle evidence.

## Milestones

### Q0 - Inventory Current Signal

Build a read-only inventory of the quality signal already in the repo.

Inputs:

- `tests/differential.rs`
- `tests/oracle_o12_x86_corpus.rs`
- `tests/oracle_o12_x86_e2e.rs`
- `tests/oracle_o13_coff_parity.rs`
- `tests/oracle_o14_omf_link.rs`
- `tests/oracle_o15_header_acceptance.rs`
- `tests/oracle_real_headers.rs`
- `tests/oracle_s4_classlib.rs`
- `tests/owl_examples_product.rs`
- `tests/railc_source_slice.rs`
- `scratchpad.md`
- relevant active `wrk_docs/*GOAL*`, `*PLAN*`, and recent journals

Output:

- A compact table of oracle, prerequisites, current floor, current known gaps,
  and whether the suite is always-on, ignored, or self-skipping.
- A list of duplicated gap classifications that should be unified.
- A list of silent or too-soft skips, if any.

Acceptance:

- No shipped code changes.
- No test rewrites.
- The inventory can be read by a future agent in under five minutes.

### Q1 - Generate The Quality Map

Create a small tool or test-side helper that runs or parses the key oracle
reports and emits a ranked backlog.

Classification dimensions:

- Area: preprocessor, parser, semantic analysis, template/dialect, codegen,
  ABI, object writer, linker, resource compiler, runtime, GUI/OWL.
- Phase reached: compile-only, object, link, launch, observable behaviour.
- Oracle strength: hand expected, differential, structural, product-path,
  fuzz-reduced, exploratory.
- Unlock value: real headers, ClassLib, OWL libraries, stock OWL examples,
  RailC, i386 parity, Win64 parity.
- Risk: crash/corruption, miscompile, link failure, unsupported syntax,
  performance, maintainability.

Ranking rule:

Prioritize by real-program unlock value times oracle strength, then break ties
by how small and local the likely fix is. Do not let easy low-value parser
acceptance work crowd out a high-value ABI/runtime bug that blocks stock OWL.

Acceptance:

- The report is deterministic.
- It prints the top 10 work items with exact source tests or reproduction
  commands.
- It separates "known reference gap" from "mdbcc gap".
- It treats skipped external prerequisites as environment state, not success.

### Q2 - Expand Stock OWL Product Coverage

Turn the current two-example product acceptance into a broader stock BC4.52 OWL
matrix.

Seed candidates:

- Already green: `OWLAPI/BUTTON/BUTTONX.CPP`, `OWLAPI/INSTANCE/INSTANCE.CPP`.
- Scratchpad failures: `OWLAPI/STATIC/STATICX.CPP`,
  `OWLAPPS/HELLO/HELLOAPP.CPP`, `OWLAPI/GROUPBOX`, `OWLAPI/EDIT`,
  `OWLAPI/GAUGE`, `OWLAPI/LISTBOX`, `OWLAPI/COMBOBOX`, `OWLAPI/SLIDER`,
  `OWLAPI/POPUP`.

Work:

- Generate normal `mdbcc.toml` manifests, not one-off compiler invocations.
- Build each app through the same Win64 product path.
- Classify the first failure per app without trying to fix everything in the
  sweep.
- For runnable apps, smoke to top-level window title and one simple interaction
  where practical.
- For GUI failures, prefer window-tree dumps and focused `PrintWindow`
  captures over full-screen screenshots.

Acceptance:

- The matrix has at least 10 stock OWL entries.
- Each entry has a stable classification and repro command.
- Existing green examples stay green.
- The output feeds Q1's ranked backlog.

### Q3 - Add A Small External Corpus Adapter

Leverage external compiler suites without flooding the repo with modern-dialect
noise.

Candidate sources:

- LLVM test-suite single-source C programs.
- GCC C torture cases, filtered to runnable C89-ish fixtures.
- Small historical C benchmark suites only when they have clear expected
  stdout/exit behaviour.

Rules:

- Start with 20-50 fixtures, not thousands.
- Prefer source files that are deterministic, input-free, UB-free, and do not
  require modern C/C++.
- Run through the existing differential model where possible: mdbcc vs MSVC
  and/or bcc32, stdout/exit after CRLF normalization.
- Keep external corpus material either vendored only when licensing permits, or
  fetched/generated into a git-ignored cache with loud skips.

Acceptance:

- One adapter runs on a curated subset and produces useful pass/fail output.
- False failures from unsupported modern language features are filtered at the
  corpus boundary, not hidden inside the compiler.
- At least one failure becomes a reduced, committed mdbcc regression test.

### Q4 - Add Fuzzing With Reduction

Use fuzzers as bug discovery tools, not as permanent noisy gates.

Candidate tools:

- Csmith for random C differential testing.
- YARPGen for codegen/optimization-style differential cases.
- C-Reduce or equivalent for reducing failures.

Rules:

- Constrain generation to the dialect and runtime surface mdbcc can honestly
  test.
- Differential targets should be mdbcc, MSVC, and bcc32 where the generated
  source is in the shared subset.
- A generated failure is not a backlog item until it has a reduced repro and a
  classification.
- Do not gate normal `cargo test` on random generation.

Acceptance:

- One end-to-end run exists: generate, detect divergence/crash, reduce, commit
  stable regression.
- The process records the generator seed and command line.
- The reduced test is small enough to debug without reading generated sludge.

### Q5 - Generate ABI And Calling-Convention Torture

Build a generator for the bug class that has hurt us most: ABI boundary
miscompiles.

Coverage:

- Win64 and i386 calls.
- Free functions, member functions, static members, virtual calls, callbacks.
- By-value structs of multiple sizes and alignments.
- Record returns and hidden sret pointers.
- Varargs and format-style calls.
- Reference and pointer parameters.
- Stack arguments after register arguments.
- Constructors, destructors, and out-of-line static member definitions.
- Two-TU caller/callee splits so cross-object contracts are tested.

Oracles:

- Same source through bcc32 where bitness permits.
- MSVC for portable Win64 shapes.
- Hand-expected checksum for shapes where no external reference is valid.

Acceptance:

- The generator emits deterministic fixture names and expected outcomes.
- At least one historical bug class is represented as a generated case.
- Failures classify by ABI feature, not by raw exit code alone.

### Q6 - Add Structural Invariants

Catch corrupt objects before a runnable executable happens to expose them.

Invariants:

- Every relocation target resolves to a known section/symbol and valid offset.
- No duplicate strong external definitions after archive resolution.
- COMDAT/weak folding choices are deterministic.
- Section alignment and image layout satisfy the target PE/COFF contract.
- x64 stack alignment and shadow-space reservations are mechanically checked at
  call sites where the IR still has enough information.
- Vtable and RTTI objects have consistent slot widths, prefixes, and
  cross-TU weak-fold names.
- EH metadata and handler references point at emitted code.

Acceptance:

- Structural checks run cheaply on focused tests.
- They produce exact object/function/symbol names on failure.
- They do not replace runtime differentials; they narrow root cause.

### Q7 - Reduce Collision Risk While Fixing Quality Gaps

Continue splitting the largest files where the quality map points.

Current risk:

- `src/codegen.rs` is large enough that unrelated fixes collide.
- `src/parser.rs` is also large enough that dialect work routinely touches the
  same broad surface.

Extraction candidates:

- Call ABI lowering and argument marshalling.
- EH lowering helpers.
- Reachability, vague linkage, and inline/prototype collision handling.
- Record layout and by-value copy/return helpers.
- Vtable/RTTI construction.
- Parser declaration/declarator forms for real headers.
- Template capture/instantiation helpers.

Rules:

- Extract one coherent region at a time.
- Preserve bytes unless intentionally fixing a bug.
- Run the narrow byte-identity and behavioural gates immediately.
- Do not mix mechanical extraction with speculative cleanup.

Acceptance:

- At least two high-collision regions move behind smaller module boundaries.
- Each extraction has byte-identity or object-IR evidence.
- Future fixes in the same area touch fewer lines and fewer unrelated concepts.

## Working rules

- Real product paths beat synthetic probes for prioritization.
- Synthetic probes are for reduction and root cause.
- External suites are signal sources, not requirements documents.
- Fuzzing output must be reduced before it becomes a bug.
- A faster wrong compiler is worse than a slow correct one.
- A green skip is not green unless the skip is loud and explicitly about a
  missing local prerequisite.
- Do not chase modern C++ conformance unless Borland-era source or an oracle
  pulls it into scope.

6. Let the first ranked backlog item drive the next actual compiler fix.

## Explicit non-goals

- Do not vendor huge third-party suites blindly.
- Do not make normal development depend on proprietary fixtures being present.
- Do not treat parse acceptance as proof of semantic support.
- Do not import a modern C++ conformance suite and call the resulting wall of
  unsupported-language failures useful.
- Do not weaken existing ratchets to make the new map look cleaner.
- Do not use the quality map as a replacement for bug-ledger discipline when a
  concrete defect is found and fixed.
