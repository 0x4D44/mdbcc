## 3. "BUGS.md" — direction of travel

**Still no `BUGS.md`** (confirmed: `git ls-files | grep -i bugs` empty). Defects continue to be tracked through the de‑facto mechanisms: the (now‑saturated) RailC link‑closure count, the `G##` gap log, known‑gap tests, Windows runtime crash codes (`0xC0000374` heap / `0xC0000005` AV), and the `MDBCC‑NN` issue list from the 06‑13 daily.

**Direction of travel — strongly positive on functionality, flat‑to‑negative on observability:**

- **Two headline defects retired in one cycle.** `MDBCC‑01` (`0xC0000374` startup heap corruption) and `MDBCC‑02` Step 1 (`0xC0000005` dispatch truncation) are both fixed *and locked behind hard panic gates*. The O3 smoke went from advisory `eprintln!` to `panic!` on recurrence of either code — regression is now loud where yesterday it was silent.
- **Bug‑class migration continued one layer up.** C++ semantics (MI/RTTI/EH) → ABI/pointer‑width (array‑new cookie width; 32‑bit `WPARAM`/`LPARAM`/`LRESULT`) → **Win64 window‑lifecycle** (dialog‑manager `WM_INITDIALOG` re‑entry hang; USER32 teardown‑ordering AV on dialog dismiss). The newest frontier is modal‑dialog integration and subclass teardown, not codegen.
- **A latent *runtime* correctness bug slipped past the "byte‑validated" 32‑bit self‑host.** The track diagram was blank because 4‑byte `float` *globals* (`XScaleFactor`/`YScaleFactor`) were over‑read with `movsd` (8‑byte) and collapsed to ~0. Fixed 06‑14 (`f91163b`, width‑correct `movss`/`cvtss2sd`). **Lesson:** byte‑identical link/resource parity does **not** prove full visual runtime behaviour — a gap in the differential‑oracle coverage worth noting.
- **New defects surfaced (in journals/notes, not a registry):** dialog dismiss‑crash `0xC0000005`; Config‑dialog no‑show; and two *unconfirmed* `-m64` codegen hypotheses (64‑bit shift‑by‑32, 64‑bit integer division).

**Recommendation (unchanged, now overdue):** stand up a lightweight `BUGS.md`. Three new items this cycle were captured only in prose. This is the single weakest part of the project's defect observability and is in direct tension with the "hard to regress" north star.

## 11. Conclusions

1. **The native‑Win64 mission is no longer "one wall away" — it has crossed the threshold.** A source‑built, mdbcc‑compiled, native‑64 OWL window renders and is interactive. M1 is genuinely done and gated. This is the headline win of the cycle.
2. **The remaining Win64 work is real but bounded.** M2 (dialogs) is mid‑flight with the hardest sub‑problem (the `WM_INITDIALOG` hang) already solved; the dismiss‑crash and Config no‑show are the next concrete targets, and Step‑2 dispatch widening — long deferred — is now being pulled in.
3. **Process discipline slipped slightly but recoverably.** Unlike yesterday's clean atomic landing, the M2 narrative is committed ahead of its code, and `main` is 1 ahead of `origin` unpushed. Neither is serious; both are worth tidying.
4. **The structural risk is unchanged and quietly growing:** a 3‑day‑unresolved north‑star split, coverage staler than ever, no `BUGS.md`, and a demonstrated blind spot (bytes ≠ pixels). Functionality is outrunning the machinery meant to keep it from regressing.
5. **A strategy call is overdue.** Native Win64 is winning by momentum, not by decision. The momentum is *good* — but it should be a choice, with conformance‑hardening explicitly parked (not merely neglected).
